Why the Store

One submission. A billion-device audience.

The Microsoft Store is the trusted front door to Windows — discovery, distribution, updates, and commerce, handled for you across Windows 10 and 11.

Reach

Hundreds of millions of customers

Your app is discoverable across the Store on every Windows 10 and 11 device, worldwide — no per-market setup.

Revenue

Keep what you earn

Bring your own commerce and keep up to 100% of your app revenue — Microsoft takes nothing.

Growth

Grow with insight

Acquisitions, usage, health, ratings and reviews — understand your audience from day one.

How it works

Three steps to published

  1. 1

    Create a developer account

    Register once in the developer portal to unlock publishing across the Microsoft Store.

  2. 2

    Package & list your app

    Reserve your app name, upload your package, and build a rich listing with screenshots and details.

  3. 3

    Submit & go live

    Pass certification and publish. Microsoft handles global distribution, hosting, and automatic updates.

Key value

Everything you need to ship and grow

From your first upload to millions of installs, the Store handles the hard parts so you can focus on building.

Reach a billion-device audience

One submission puts your app in front of customers on every Windows 10 and 11 device, worldwide.

Keep up to 100% of revenue

Bring your own commerce and Microsoft takes nothing — or use Microsoft's and keep 85% (88% for PC games).

Effortless distribution & updates

Microsoft hosts your packages on a global CDN and delivers automatic updates to every user.

Built-in analytics

Track acquisitions, usage, health, and ratings & reviews right from the developer portal.

Trusted & secure by default

Every app is certified and malware-scanned, so customers install with confidence.

One identity, any app

A single developer account ships Win32, MSIX, .NET, PWA, or Electron — any framework that runs on Windows.

Loved by developers
