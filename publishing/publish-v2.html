      Download starter AppxManifest.xml Copy <Identity> snippet       How to build your package

      Package your app as an MSIX (.msix / .msixbundle) and drop it in the box above. Already have a .exe / .msi installer? Bring it as-is, or convert it to MSIX after upload. To build an MSIX, pick how:

      1. Install the Windows SDK. It gives you makeappx and signtool.
      2. Set your identity. In AppxManifest.xml, match the Name and Publisher to your reservation.
      3. Build.makeappx pack /d YourAppFolder /p App.msix
      4. Sign.signtool sign /fd SHA256 /a /f cert.pfx App.msix
      5. Upload the signed App.msix in the box above.
      Full walkthrough
