What is WDP

One program for verified developers on Windows

The Windows Developer Program is the umbrella for becoming a verified developer on Windows — earning a Blue Badge that builds trust with users. Verify by publishing to the Microsoft Store or by signing your apps with a certificate — same badge, either way.

The umbrella

One developer identity

Identity, app authenticity, and reputation come together under one verified developer profile — however you choose to ship.
